"Furtherance" Information

If IRCC has sent you a request for further medical information

Don't panic! Don't ignore the letter.

Please email frontdesk@aimsgta.ca (if you did your medical in Etobicoke) or maltondesk@aimsgta.ca (if you did your medical in Mississauga) with your UMI or IME number, and indicate you received a request for further medical information.

Our staff will book you in for any required tests. This may include bloodwork, x-rays, sputum tests for tuberculosis, or medical specialist reports. Note that tests may have associated fees.
